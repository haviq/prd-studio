FAQ

Pricing questions.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Guests get 1 PRD per day with no account. A free GitHub account raises it to 10 per day and lets you save projects.

What happens when I hit the limit?

You will see a message asking you to sign in or upgrade. The limit resets daily.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is month-to-month and you can cancel whenever you like.

Do you offer team or self-hosted plans?

Yes, that is our Enterprise plan. Contact sales and we will set it up.
