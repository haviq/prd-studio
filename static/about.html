About

Specs should not take days.

PRD Studio exists for one reason: the blank page. Writing a product requirements document is the slowest part of shipping, so we made it a one-minute job.

What it is

PRD Studio is an AI product spec generator. You describe a product in a sentence and it writes a complete, nine-to-thirteen section PRD: overview, scope, features and user stories, architecture, API design, data model, security, testing, deployment and roadmap, plus an architecture diagram and an ERD.

Who it is for

Founders, indie developers, product managers and small teams who need to turn an idea into something a builder can act on. It is deliberately simple: no templates to wrangle, no blank page, no setup.

How it works

The studio sends your brief to a large language model that drafts the document section by section, then generates the diagrams. Everything runs on our own infrastructure, and self-hosting is available on the Enterprise plan.

Pricing philosophy

You can try it free without an account. Signing in with GitHub raises the daily limit and lets you save and revise projects. Pro removes the limits entirely. If a spec saves you an afternoon, it has paid for itself.

Who builds it

PRD Studio is built and maintained by an independent developer in Indonesia. Questions, feedback or a bug report? Email founder@haaviq.dev.

Output is AI-generated. Treat every document as a strong first draft and verify anything critical before you rely on it.
